# M2-210 — Final gates and approval flow

## What was built
Final compliance gates F1–F6 (`internal/compliance/final.go`) and the approval scheduling service
(`internal/content/approval.go`). On final pass, `ApprovalService.Start` creates a pending approval and
enqueues `approval.request`. Telegram and HTTP dashboard decisions route through `ApprovalService.Decide`
so approve schedules `publications` within cadence caps, reject stops, and redo enqueues `script.write`.
`RequireApproved` is the publisher base gate for M2-211+.
