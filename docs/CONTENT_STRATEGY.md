**Money & Side Hustles:** budgeting systems, credit and debt basics, investing concepts (index funds, compounding — educational only), taxes explained simply, side hustles with real numbers, online business case studies, money psychology, scams to avoid.

## 4. Craft rules for scripts

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds** (Shorts) / first 15 seconds (long): a specific promise or surprising fact.
- One idea per Short; 30–50 seconds; payoff before the end; loopable last line.
- Long videos 8–12 minutes, chapters every 1–2 minutes, a pattern change (visual or format beat) every 20–40 s.
- Title ≤ 60 characters, thumbnail text ≤ 4 words, both must match what the video delivers.
- End with one call to action (subscribe, or the related long video) — never both.

## 6. Growth loop (after week 4)

Metrics pulled at +24h and +7d: views, average view %, CTR, subs gained per video, shares. Scores kept per **topic cluster, format, hook style, time slot**. The Scout ranks new topics by predicted score; the format picker prefers winners but keeps ~20% exploration so the channel doesn't become repetitive.

## 7. Niche review

Every 2 weeks the dashboard shows each channel's trend. If a channel is flat for 6 weeks despite changes, propose a pivot (backup niches: Psychology & Self-improvement, History & Documentaries) — Mayank decides.
